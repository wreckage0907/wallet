Wallet
Documentation Support Source

Your bank statements, parsed, de-duplicated and finally readable.

Wallet is a personal finance app for Frappe. Add your bank accounts, drop in the statement files your bank emails you, and Wallet parses, de-duplicates and categorizes every row, then shows you where the money went, on your phone.

Install Read the docs

What it does

Imports real bank files

Encrypted xlsx, branch details above the table, a totals row below. Wallet finds the transactions, maps the columns, and stages every row for review before anything is written.

De-duplicates overlapping statements

Each transaction is fingerprinted from its own content, so re-importing an overlapping month adds nothing twice.

Categorizes from your rules

Ships with rules tuned for Indian bank narrations (Swiggy, Zomato, FASTag, NACH EMIs, salary credits) and a two-level category tree you can change.

Balances that cannot drift

Every balance is recomputed from the opening balance and the transactions, never kept as a running counter.

Reconciles every import

After an import, Wallet compares its closing balance with the one your statement states, so a mis-read row cannot pass quietly.

Works with your AI assistant

Ask what you spent on groceries last month through Frappe's built-in MCP server. Changes need both your administrator and you to switch them on.

Reconciliation you can see

Your data stays on your site

Wallet runs inside your own Frappe site. Statements and transactions are stored in that site's database and nowhere else. The app has no analytics, telemetry or phone-home of any kind, and every record is visible only to the person who created it. Read the privacy policy.

Get started

Wallet needs a Frappe v16.53.0 or later site.

bench get-app https://github.com/wreckage0907/wallet --branch main
bench --site <site> install-app wallet
bench build --app wallet

Then assign the Wallet User role and open /wallet. The installation guide has the details.
